# Blender pass 2: furniture, judged against Tier S

Date: 2026-10-21. Script: `tools/blender/build_bridge.py` (furniture
section). Renders in this folder, same four cameras as pass 1.

## Verdict

The bridge now has its furniture silhouette. Against frame 01 the
command chair trio sits on the burgundy oval inside a solid wooden
horseshoe rail, with the aft station wall running continuously behind
it and turbolift doors in the corners. Against frame 05 the Conn and
Ops chairs and their black sloped console tops sit under the viewer
at the right spacing.

## What was added

- Three command chairs (captain wider) built from pedestal, seat,
  ribbed back, headrest, and armrest pads.
- Horseshoe rail: a wood tube over a solid curved ribbon, with the
  tactical console at its aft centre.
- Conn and Ops: reclined chairs plus tan consoles with glossy black
  sloped tops and a small emissive LCARS strip each.
- Aft station wall: five continuous units (cabinet, dark sloped
  display, black upper band, glow strip) tight against the shell on
  the raised deck.

## Known gaps for pass 3

- Chairs are still boxes. The set chairs are sculpted shells with
  padded ribbing and shaped armrest consoles.
- LCARS is blank emissive strips. Real LCARS art (recreated, not
  cropped from stills) is the materials pass.
- Rail ends stop mid-air at the forward ends; on the set they curve
  down to the floor.
- Viewer surround corners are still square, doors still lack padded
  alcoves and labels, and the dome ribs are still straight spokes
  rather than arched geodesic panels.

## Score

About 6.5 to 7/10 overall. The remaining distance is mostly surface
detail and chair sculpting, not layout.
